`StreamChat` and `StreamChatPopups` in `@atmo-dev/kit/streamplace`: live chat for a `Channel` with replies, reactions, mentions, link embeds and cards, message ages and the streamer's moderation actions, and brief popups for new messages while the chat is hidden. `streamLinkEmbed()` shows channel links as cards. `Channel.receive()` applies events an app supplies itself.
