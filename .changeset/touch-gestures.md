Touch gestures: mobile drawers (`IconRail` with `mobile="drawer"`, `WorkspaceSidebar`) follow a finger, opening with a swipe right on the page and closing with a swipe left, and slide out when dismissed (`swipe={false}` opts out); `PhotoLightbox` closes when a photo is pulled down; new `PullToRefresh` component; new `swipeBack` helper for pages that go back instead of opening a drawer. `Modal` exposes `bind:ref` and `bind:overlayRef`.
